# Oída Roadmap

Oída 0.6.5 is a public alpha and open research release. This roadmap describes
the questions being worked on; it is not a promise of dates or production
stability.

## Working Now

- Model-free deterministic listening through the dashboard, CLI, REST, and
  MCP surfaces.
- Optional local MOSS-Audio and declared host-perception paths.
- AKOÚŌ claim categories, route presets, apparatus, and listening covenants.
- Explicit memory, Akousmata navigation, and sound, prompt, or lineage handoff
  to GERM.
- Event-grounded discussion that cannot rewrite the source listening event.
- A shared web dashboard and native macOS shell.

## Next Research Priorities

- Expand license-safe signal and route fixtures across field, music, voice,
  tonal, percussive, and low-information material.
- Publish a clearer tested-platform matrix for model-free, Apple Silicon, and
  operator-managed CUDA paths.
- Improve keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, and low-vision coverage
  across the dashboard and floating listener.
- Strengthen round-trip lineage tests across Oída, GERM, Earworm, and
  Akousmata.
- Improve long-recording segmentation while preserving uncertainty at chunk
  boundaries.
- Make model, apparatus, and provider attribution easier to export with a
  listening record.

## Toward a Stable Contract

- Stabilize the gateway and host-perception schemas from public use and
  conformance fixtures.
- Document migrations for durable sessions, compatibility traces, and shared
  akousma records.
- Broaden reproducible packaging beyond the source-based macOS shell.
- Establish repeatable performance and memory reporting across supported
  checkpoints and hardware.

## Non-Goals

Oída is not intended to certify that a model heard correctly, infer consent
from audio, publish a listening service to the internet, or make cloud models
local by description. Local-first is an operating boundary, not an automatic
ethical guarantee.
